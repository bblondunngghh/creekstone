Roofing Systems

Commercial Roofing Systems We Install

Waterloo Roofing Co is experienced with every major commercial roofing system. We help you select the right material based on your building type, budget, energy goals, and long-term maintenance requirements.

TPO Single-Ply

Thermoplastic polyolefin is the most popular commercial roofing material in Austin. White TPO membranes reflect solar heat, reducing cooling costs by up to 30% in Central Texas summers. TPO is lightweight, resistant to punctures and chemical exposure, and carries manufacturer warranties up to 30 years. Ideal for flat and low-slope roofs.

EPDM Rubber

EPDM (ethylene propylene diene monomer) rubber roofing is a proven, cost-effective solution for commercial flat roofs. EPDM resists UV degradation, ozone, and weathering with a lifespan of 20 to 30 years. Available in black or white, it installs quickly and performs reliably in Austin's temperature extremes from freezing winters to 105-degree summers.

Modified Bitumen

Modified bitumen roofing combines the durability of built-up roofing with modern polymer technology. Multi-layer application provides excellent waterproofing and puncture resistance for roofs with heavy foot traffic or rooftop equipment. SBS-modified systems remain flexible in cold weather while APP-modified systems excel in Austin's intense heat.

Standing Seam Metal

Standing seam metal roofing offers the longest lifespan of any commercial system at 40 to 60 years. Concealed fasteners eliminate leak points, and reflective coatings reduce energy costs. Metal roofing stands up to Austin's hailstorms, high winds, and UV exposure. It is ideal for offices, retail, and buildings where long-term value is the priority.

Built-Up Roofing (BUR)

Built-up roofing systems use alternating layers of bitumen and reinforcing fabric to create a durable, multi-ply membrane. BUR has been used on commercial buildings for over a century and provides excellent waterproofing and fire resistance. Gravel-surfaced BUR reflects heat and protects the membrane from UV damage in the Austin sun.

Roof Coatings

Elastomeric and silicone roof coatings extend the life of existing commercial roofs by 10 to 15 years at a fraction of the cost of full replacement. Coatings seal minor cracks, reflect solar heat, and provide a seamless waterproof barrier. They are an excellent option for roofs that are aging but structurally sound, saving Austin businesses significant capital expense.

Our Process

Our Commercial Roofing Process

Every commercial roofing project follows a structured process designed to deliver quality results on time and within budget while minimizing disruption to your business.

1

Comprehensive Assessment

We begin with a thorough inspection of your existing roof, including core samples when necessary to evaluate the condition of underlying layers. We assess the building's structural capacity, drainage patterns, rooftop equipment, and any code compliance requirements specific to your Austin location.

2

Detailed Proposal

You receive a comprehensive proposal outlining the recommended roofing system, material specifications, warranty options, project timeline, and a transparent line-by-line cost breakdown. We present multiple options when appropriate so you can make an informed decision based on your budget and long-term goals.

3

Scheduling Around Your Business

We coordinate with your operations team to develop a project schedule that minimizes disruption. This includes phasing the work to keep occupied areas protected, scheduling noisy activities during off-peak hours, and planning around your delivery schedules, events, and peak business periods.

4

Professional Installation

Our experienced commercial crews follow manufacturer specifications and industry best practices on every project. We maintain a clean, organized job site with proper safety protocols. Daily progress updates keep you informed, and our project manager is your single point of contact throughout the installation.

5

Final Inspection

Upon completion, we perform a detailed quality inspection of the entire roof system. We verify proper seam welds, flashing details, drainage function, and equipment curb seals. You receive a final walkthrough and all warranty documentation before we consider the project complete.

6

Maintenance Plan

We offer ongoing preventative maintenance plans that include bi-annual inspections, drain clearing, sealant maintenance, and minor repairs. Regular maintenance extends your commercial roof's lifespan by years, reduces emergency repair costs, and keeps your manufacturer warranty in good standing.
