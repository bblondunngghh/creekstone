When to Inspect

When You Need a Roof Inspection

Regular roof inspections catch small problems before they become expensive repairs. Here are the most common situations where a professional inspection protects your investment.

Buying a Home

A pre-purchase roof inspection reveals the true condition of the roof before you close on a property. Knowing whether the roof has 2 years or 15 years of remaining life directly affects your negotiating position and helps you budget for future expenses. Austin's competitive housing market makes this step essential.

After a Storm

Austin experiences severe hailstorms and high winds that cause roof damage invisible from the ground. After any significant weather event, a professional inspection documents hail impacts, lifted shingles, damaged flashing, and compromised seals. This documentation is critical for filing insurance claims within your policy's deadline.

Roof Aging

Asphalt shingle roofs in Austin typically last 15 to 25 years depending on material quality and maintenance. If your roof is approaching the 15-year mark, an annual inspection identifies early signs of wear such as granule loss, curling edges, and deteriorating pipe boots before they lead to active leaks.

Before Selling

A pre-sale roof inspection gives you leverage during the selling process. If the roof is in good condition, you have documentation to reassure buyers. If repairs are needed, addressing them before listing prevents surprises during the buyer's inspection and avoids renegotiation that could delay or derail the sale.

Insurance Requirement

Many homeowners insurance providers in Texas require a roof inspection before issuing or renewing a policy, especially for roofs over 10 years old. Our inspection reports meet insurance company requirements and provide the documentation needed to maintain your coverage without interruption.

A well-maintained roof after professional inspection — protecting your Austin home for years to come.

Our Process

Our 21-Point Inspection Process

Waterloo RoofCo follows a comprehensive 21-point inspection process that evaluates every critical component of your roofing system. Nothing gets overlooked.

1

Exterior Assessment

We begin with a ground-level and roof-level visual assessment of the entire roof surface. This includes checking for sagging ridgelines, uneven planes, and visible damage. We photograph the roof from multiple angles to establish baseline documentation of its current condition.

2

Shingle and Material Evaluation

We inspect every section of roofing material for granule loss, cracking, curling, blistering, and hail impacts. On shingle roofs, we check for proper nail placement and adequate overlap. We note the manufacturer, approximate age, and remaining useful life of the materials.

3

Flashing and Penetrations

Every roof penetration is a potential leak point. We inspect flashing around chimneys, skylights, plumbing vents, exhaust fans, and wall-to-roof transitions. We check for cracked caulking, rusted metal, lifted edges, and improper installation that could allow water entry.

4

Gutters and Drainage

Proper drainage protects both the roof and the foundation. We inspect gutters for alignment, debris buildup, rust, and secure attachment. Downspouts are checked for proper routing away from the foundation. We note any areas where water may be pooling on the roof surface.

5

Attic and Ventilation

From inside the attic, we check for signs of water intrusion including stains, mold, and damaged insulation. We evaluate ventilation by inspecting soffit vents, ridge vents, and any powered ventilators. Inadequate ventilation in Austin's heat accelerates shingle deterioration and drives up energy costs.

6

Structural Assessment

We examine the roof decking for soft spots, sagging, and delamination. Rafters and trusses are checked for signs of stress, cracking, or water damage. Any structural concerns are flagged immediately as they affect the safety and integrity of the entire roofing system.

7

Written Report with Photos

Within 24 hours of your inspection, you receive a comprehensive written report that includes photographs of every finding, a condition rating for each component, and clear recommendations. If repairs or replacement are needed, the report explains the urgency and estimated cost so you can make an informed decision.

What's Included in Your Report

Every Waterloo RoofCo inspection report is designed to give you a complete, transparent picture of your roof's condition. Here is what you receive.

01

High-Resolution Photos

Every area of concern is documented with clear, labeled photographs taken during the inspection. You see exactly what we see, from granule loss close-ups to wide shots of overall roof condition.

02

Component Ratings

Each part of your roofing system receives a condition rating: good, fair, or needs attention. This makes it easy to understand the overall health of your roof at a glance and prioritize any needed repairs.

03

Repair Recommendations

If issues are found, your report includes specific repair recommendations with estimated costs and urgency levels. We distinguish between items that need immediate attention and those that can be monitored over time.

04

Remaining Roof Life Estimate

Based on the material type, current condition, and Austin's climate factors, we provide an estimated remaining lifespan for your roof. This helps you plan for future expenses and make informed decisions about repair versus replacement.

05

Insurance-Ready Documentation

For storm damage inspections, our reports include the detailed documentation insurance companies require: date-stamped photos, damage descriptions, affected areas mapped on a roof diagram, and material specifications needed for accurate claim processing.

06

Maintenance Recommendations

Your report concludes with a maintenance schedule tailored to your roof's material and condition. Proactive maintenance extends roof life and helps you avoid costly emergency repairs down the road.
