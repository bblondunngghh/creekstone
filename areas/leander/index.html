Your Local Leander Roofing Contractor

Leander is one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States, and Waterloo Roofing Co is proud to serve its booming community of homeowners and businesses. Located in Williamson County northwest of Austin, Leander has transformed from a quiet Hill Country town into a thriving suburban city — defined by a mix of brand-new master-planned subdivisions and the rugged terrain that gives the Texas Hill Country its character.

That rapid growth brings unique roofing challenges. New construction homes need precise installation from day one, while the area's limestone terrain and weather patterns — including frequent spring hailstorms — demand materials and workmanship that can hold up long-term. With the Capital MetroRail connecting Leander directly to downtown Austin, the city continues to attract families who expect quality in every investment, including their roof. Waterloo Roofing Co delivers just that: expert craftsmanship, transparent pricing, and service built around Leander's family-oriented community.
