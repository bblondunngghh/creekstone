Your Local Georgetown Roofing Contractor

Georgetown is the county seat of Williamson County and one of the most distinctive cities in Central Texas. Known for its beautifully preserved Victorian downtown square — recognized as one of the most beautiful in Texas — Georgetown balances a rich historic identity with some of the fastest population growth in the entire country. The Sun City retirement community has brought thousands of new residents to the area, while master-planned developments continue expanding Georgetown's footprint in every direction.

This blend of historic homes and new construction creates a unique range of roofing needs. Older properties near the downtown square require careful, detail-oriented workmanship that respects original architecture and may fall under historic district guidelines. Newer homes in Sun City, Berry Creek, and Cimarron Hills often prioritize energy efficiency, impact resistance, and HOA compliance. Waterloo RoofCo has the experience to serve both — with the same high standards, transparent pricing, and reliable communication on every job.
