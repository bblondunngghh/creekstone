Austin Weather

How Austin's Climate Affects Your Roof

Austin's climate presents specific challenges that affect roofing material selection, installation methods, and maintenance schedules:

  • Hail: Austin averages 3–5 significant hail events per year, primarily from March through June. Large hail (1 inch or greater) causes impact damage to shingles, cracking tiles, and denting metal flashing and vents.
  • Heat: Summer temperatures regularly exceed 100°F, with roof surface temperatures reaching 150–170°F. This accelerates shingle aging, causes thermal expansion damage, and degrades adhesive seals.
  • UV Exposure: Austin receives approximately 2,650 hours of sunshine annually. Intense UV radiation breaks down asphalt shingle granules over time, reducing waterproofing effectiveness.
  • Wind: Severe thunderstorms bring wind gusts of 60–80+ mph. Improperly installed shingles and ridge caps are vulnerable to wind uplift and blow-off.
  • Heavy Rain: Austin averages 34 inches of annual rainfall, often concentrated in intense storm events. Proper drainage, flashing, and gutter systems are essential to prevent water intrusion.

Creekstone Roof Co specifies materials and installation methods based on these conditions. We recommend Class 4 impact-resistant shingles for most Austin homes, which resist hail damage and qualify for insurance premium discounts of up to 28% with many Texas carriers.
