About Waterloo Roofing Co

A locally owned Austin roofing company built on transparency, quality craftsmanship, and genuine care for the homes and businesses we serve.

Our Story

Why We Started Waterloo Roofing Co

Waterloo Roofing Co was founded in Austin, TX with a simple premise: homeowners deserve a roofing contractor they can trust. Too many Austin families have been burned by storm chasers who roll into town after hail, collect insurance money, and disappear — leaving behind shoddy work and voided warranties.

We built Waterloo to be the opposite of that. We are locally based in Austin. We use our real name, our real address, and we stand behind every roof we install. When you call Waterloo, you speak with someone who lives in your community and has a personal stake in the quality of the work.

The name "Waterloo" reflects our roots. Before Austin was Austin, it was Waterloo — the original settlement on the Colorado River that would grow into the capital of Texas. We chose the name because it represents the foundation this city was built on: resilience, independence, and a commitment to building something that lasts. That is how we approach every roof we install — with the kind of permanence and craftsmanship that honors the place we call home.

Our Values

What Waterloo Roofing Co Stands For

Radical Transparency

Every estimate is line-by-line. Every material choice is explained. Every cost is justified. We never rely on confusion or pressure to close a sale. You see exactly what you are paying for and why — before any work begins.

No Shortcuts

We use manufacturer-certified materials on every project. Our crews are trained — not subcontracted day laborers picked up that morning. We follow manufacturer installation specifications exactly, which is what keeps your warranty valid.

Community First

We live and work in Austin. Our kids go to Austin schools. We shop at Austin businesses. When we put a roof on your home, we are investing in our own community — and that accountability shows in the quality of our work.

Working With Us

What to Expect as a Waterloo Customer

Every Waterloo customer receives the same level of care, regardless of project size. Here is what working with us looks like:

  • Same-day or next-business-day response to every inquiry — phone, email, or online form
