About Creekstone Roof Co

A locally owned Austin roofing company built on transparency, quality craftsmanship, and genuine care for the homes and businesses we serve.

Our Story

Why We Started Creekstone Roof Co

Creekstone Roof Co was founded in Austin, TX with a simple premise: homeowners deserve a roofing contractor they can trust. Too many Austin families have been burned by storm chasers who roll into town after hail, collect insurance money, and disappear — leaving behind shoddy work and voided warranties.

We built Creekstone to be the opposite of that. We are locally based in Austin. We use our real name, our real address, and we stand behind every roof we install. When you call Creekstone, you speak with someone who lives in your community and has a personal stake in the quality of the work.

The name "Creekstone" reflects our philosophy. Like the limestone bedrock that defines the Austin landscape — from the Barton Creek Greenbelt to the Hill Country bluffs — we believe a roof should be built on a solid foundation of honest work, quality materials, and clear communication. Nothing hidden, nothing temporary, nothing that washes away in the next storm.

Our Values

What Creekstone Roof Co Stands For

Radical Transparency

Every estimate is line-by-line. Every material choice is explained. Every cost is justified. We never rely on confusion or pressure to close a sale. You see exactly what you are paying for and why — before any work begins.

No Shortcuts

We use manufacturer-certified materials on every project. Our crews are trained — not subcontracted day laborers picked up that morning. We follow manufacturer installation specifications exactly, which is what keeps your warranty valid.

Community First

We live and work in Austin. Our kids go to Austin schools. We shop at Austin businesses. When we put a roof on your home, we are investing in our own community — and that accountability shows in the quality of our work.

Working With Us

What to Expect as a Creekstone Customer

Every Creekstone customer receives the same level of care, regardless of project size. Here is what working with us looks like:

  • Same-day or next-business-day response to every inquiry — phone, email, or online form
  • Free, no-obligation inspection with a written report and photos
  • Detailed, line-by-line estimate explaining every material and cost
  • Zero pressure sales approach — we present options and let you decide on your timeline
